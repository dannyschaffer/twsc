BlogEP 107: Unshakeable: How Jeremy Aguiar Plays the Long Game in Day Trading

Mindset & Psychology

🎧 Listen to this Episode

Also available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and YouTube

In this powerful episode of The Wall Street Coach Podcast, Kim Ann Curtin welcomes trader Jeremy Aguiar. With 17 years of experience—starting at one of Chicago’s largest prop firms before going solo—Jeremy brings a quiet depth to the trading community. In this conversation, Kim and Jeremy explore the mental flexibility, self-awareness, and resilience required to survive and thrive in the markets.

Learn why the “all or nothing” mentality can hurt your growth, how to build non-negotiable habits, and why trading success often comes down to understanding yourself more than the markets.
