Key Takeaways

Stop Making Resolutions: Conduct a Needs Inventory to Fix Your Trading

New Year's resolutions rarely stick and often leave traders feeling like failures, compounding emotional damage and self-judgment. If you want sustainable change in your performance, you must stop focusing on surface-level goals and address the deep psychological drivers sabotaging your success.

Key Insights: - Identify Your Basic Needs: Feelings like depression, anger, and apathy are "dashboard lights" indicating your core human needs (e.g., security, freedom, acceptance) are going unmet.

  • Needs Drive Market Behavior: Understanding your own needs gives you crucial insight into the motives of other market participants, providing a psychological edge.
  • Be a Selective Steward: The market cannot reliably meet your core needs. You must intentionally identify reliable sources outside of your trading to satisfy your non-negotiable emotional requirements.Actionable Takeaway:

Conduct a "needs inventory" for yourself, identifying 2-3 non-negotiable needs that were insufficiently met this past year, and develop an intentional plan to address them."Those feelings aren't bad or wrong they are just like lights on the dashboard of you your own vehicle that you are in need of one or more of these needs."
