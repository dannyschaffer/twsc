Stop Fighting Your Feelings: The Practice of Emotional Nonresistance

When uncomfortable feelings arise—especially after losses—most traders immediately resist or suppress them, creating a cycle of psychological damage. This resistance prevents genuine emotional processing, leaving old pain stuck in the body to resurface repeatedly, ultimately keeping you from achieving peak performance. True mastery comes from learning how to surf the emotional wave instead of fighting it. * Emotional Nonresistance is Like Surfing a Wave: Instead of fighting unpleasant feelings, you must stay present and ride the emotion fully to the shore, allowing the cycle to complete.

  • Trauma Keeps You Stuck: Unprocessed emotional incidents, even small ones, interrupt the brain's ability to resolve the experience, leading to continuous, repetitive emotional responses.
  • Feelings are Physical: Emotions live in the body (somatic experience) and cannot be released until they are fully felt and processed, making awareness of physical tension critical.Practice being the neutral observer of any uncomfortable emotion that arises today, noting it without judgment or trying to force a positive shift.Feelings and emotions live in the body and the only way they come out of our body is when we feel that.
