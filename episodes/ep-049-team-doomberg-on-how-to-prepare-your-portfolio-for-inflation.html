The Emotional Cost of Denying Macro Reality During Inflation

Navigating periods of high inflation and aggressive rate hikes requires confronting hard truths about global market flows and central bank resolve. When investors prioritize ideological belief or wishful thinking over objective economic data, they expose their portfolio and their psychological resilience to immense risk. Team Doomberg breaks down the macro currents—from Federal Reserve policy to China’s massive reopening—and reveals where market complacency is most dangerous.
