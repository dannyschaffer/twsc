Episode 22: Holding Space for Yourself

Key Takeaways

How to Hold Space for Yourself When Past Trauma Hijacks Your Mindset

The market is not the only source of volatility. Sometimes, a high-stakes moment triggers old, unresolved trauma, leaving you in a debilitating state of hyper-vigilance and emotional spiraling—even after the event is financially resolved. This episode explores why this happens even to the most self-aware traders and outlines the critical psychological work required to move through it.

Key Insights - \*\*Acknowledge vs. Numb\*\*: Ignoring or compartmentalizing emotional triggers prevents healing and forces the trauma to be stored in the body, leading to continued emotional costs.

  • \*\*Shift Your Inquiry\*\*: Instead of getting caught in the self-critical cycle of asking "Why did I do that," shift your focus objectively to "What is going on for me right now."
  • \*\*Trauma Takes Time\*\*: Even once you identify a PTSD trigger, recovery is a process that requires ongoing gentleness and self-care, not instant resolution.Actionable Takeaway
