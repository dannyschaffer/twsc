The Grace of Ownership: Why Self-Responsibility is the Antidote to Trading Victimhood

In trading and in life, the ego despises being wrong, leading you to hold onto losing positions and create narratives of victimhood rather than acceptance. This emotional cost cripples your ability to grow and prevents you from accessing your power. Self-responsibility is the foundational practice that liberates you from self-blame and allows you to consciously design your trading reality.

Key Insights: - \*\*Self-Responsibility is a Declaration, Not Blame\*\*: True responsibility means taking the stand that you are the cause in the matter of your life; it is separated from fault, shame, or guilt.
