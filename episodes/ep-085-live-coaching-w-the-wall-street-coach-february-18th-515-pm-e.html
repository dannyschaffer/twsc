Key Takeaways

Stop Doubting Your Strategy: Unlocking the Answers Within

When persistent trading issues like FOMO or fear of risk haunt your decision-making, it often stems from internal psychological blocks, not flawed charts. These emotional deterrents make you question yourself and prevent you from trading your strategy effectively. * Coaching is about accessing your own internal wisdom, not about being "fixed" by an external expert.

  • Long-term losses can create limiting beliefs that make you afraid to execute even winning strategies.
  • The power of coaching comes from having someone truly listen, allowing you to hear and confront your self-doubt for the first time.Actionable Takeaway: Identify one specific trading issue (like FOMO or loss aversion) that is causing you to doubt your strategy and commit to listening to your own inner voice instead of your inner critic.My job as a coach is to ask questions that open you up to your own internal wisdom.
