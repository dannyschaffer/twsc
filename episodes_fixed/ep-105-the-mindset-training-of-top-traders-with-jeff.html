EP 105: The Mindset & Training of Top Traders with Jeff Holden of SMB Capital

🎧 Listen to this Episode

Also available on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and YouTube

On this episode of thePerformance Coach for Traders Podcast, we sit down withJeff Holden, a seasoned trader atSMB Capitaland the firm’sHead of Recruiting. Over the past six years, Jeff has not only honed his own trading skills but has also played a pivotal role indeveloping top talent.

Jeff shares how his passion for mentorship grew naturally, especially as he started thinking beyond his own success and focusing onhelping other traders grow. During the pandemic, SMB was forced to scale its training globally, leading to the creation ofSMB Training, a system designed tohelp traders improve at scale.

Jeff dives into what separatessuccessful traders from those who struggle, whyrepeatable processes matter more than short-term wins, and how traders can builda true edgein the markets.
